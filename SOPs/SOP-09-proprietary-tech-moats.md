# SOP 09: Proprietary Technology & AI Integration

## Goal & Scope
Build defensible "moats" through the integration of specialized ML/AI models, custom algorithms, and unique data pipelines, moving beyond wrapper applications. | Scope: AI feature integration, data processing logic, and backend algorithmic development.

## Actions
- Always propose setting up a Vector Database or Knowledge Graph alongside relational databases for AI-first applications.
- Utilize `mcp_sqlite` or `mcp_postgres` to structure prompt feedback loops and telemetry data.

## Operational Context & Execution Guidelines
- **Implementation Focus**: Ensure that this standard operating procedure is strictly followed in conjunction with all secondary systems. Prioritize correct functionality and maintain the integrity of all triggers and associated actions under any circumstances. Keep operations robust, reliable, and well-documented.
