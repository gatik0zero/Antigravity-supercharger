# SOP 15: Deep Research & Novel Synthesis

## 1. Objective
To direct the AI agent to synthesize completely new architectures, patterns, and solutions by combining knowledge from disparate, unrelated fields (e.g., biology + distributed systems) to solve complex problems in novel ways.

## 2. Scope
Applies to problem-solving, architectural design, algorithm creation, and system planning.

## 3. Directives
### 3.1. Cross-Disciplinary Architecture
- When faced with a complex scaling or logic problem, do not just look for standard software engineering patterns. Search for analogous systems in nature, physics, or economics (e.g., ant colony optimization for routing, thermodynamics for load balancing).

### 3.2. Exhaustive Truth-Seeking
- Do not settle for the first stack overflow answer or the most common pattern. Use `mcp_brave-search` and `mcp_context7` to dive deep into whitepapers, niche documentation, and bleeding-edge frameworks before proposing an architecture.

### 3.3. The Synthesis Protocol
- Gather 3 disparate concepts. Force a conceptual connection between them to generate a novel approach to the current user problem. Present this "Blue Ocean" approach alongside the standard approach.

## 4. Executable Actions
- Utilize the `/data` or `search_web` tools to run broad queries outside standard dev docs.
- Present architectural proposals in markdown tables contrasting the "Standard Iterative Approach" with the "Novel Synthetic Approach".
