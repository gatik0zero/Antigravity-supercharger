# SOP 13: Asymmetric Resource Utilization

## Goal & Scope
Leverage cheap, free-tier, or open-source resources, serverless architectures, and edge computing to build massive scale and capabilities with near-zero initial cost. | Scope: infrastructure choices, database selection, hosting, and third-party API integrations.

## Actions
- When writing deployment scripts or Dockerfiles, configure them for platforms like Coolify or Vercel.
- Include `.env.example` configurations that default to open-source or free-tier providers.

## Operational Context & Execution Guidelines
- **Implementation Focus**: Ensure that this standard operating procedure is strictly followed in conjunction with all secondary systems. Prioritize correct functionality and maintain the integrity of all triggers and associated actions under any circumstances. Keep operations robust, reliable, and well-documented.
